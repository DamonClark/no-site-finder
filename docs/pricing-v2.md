# Pricing Strategy V2

## Current Stage

Validation.

Primary goal:

Acquire first paying customers.

Pricing optimization is secondary.

---

## Future Pricing Model

### Free

$0

Includes:

* Limited searches
* Limited exports
* Basic opportunity scoring

Purpose:

Allow users to experience value quickly.

---

### Solo

$49/month

Includes:

* 100 searches/month
* Opportunity scoring
* Lead lists
* Notes
* Contact pipeline
* CSV export

Target:

Freelancers

Solo web designers

---

### Agency

$99/month

Includes:

* Unlimited searches
* Team access
* Saved searches
* Advanced filters
* Priority support

Target:

Small agencies

---

## Email Discovery

Separate credit system.

Reason:

Direct lookup costs.

Example:

100 credits = $19

500 credits = $69

Only enrichment consumes credits.

Searches should not consume credits.

---

## Positioning

Do not compete on lowest price.

Compete on:

Lead quality

Website confidence

Agency workflow

---

## Future Metrics

Monitor:

Trial -> Paid

Activation Rate

Searches Per User

Leads Saved

Leads Contacted

Customer Retention

Adjust pricing only after usage data exists.

Success Metric:

Customers consistently receive more value than monthly subscription cost.
