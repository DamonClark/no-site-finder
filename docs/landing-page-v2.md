# Landing Page V2 Spec

## Goal

Improve positioning and credibility.

Current landing page feels AI-generated and feature-focused.

Future landing page should focus on outcomes.

Users do not want leads.

Users want clients.

---

## Target Customer

Primary:

* Freelance web designers
* Small web design agencies

Secondary:

* SEO agencies
* Marketing agencies

---

## Core Promise

Find businesses without websites.

Turn them into clients.

---

## Hero Section

Headline:

Find Local Businesses Without Websites

Subheadline:

Discover high-opportunity prospects, prioritize the best leads, and track outreach from one platform.

CTA:

Start Free

Secondary CTA:

View Sample Leads

---

## Social Proof Section

Future additions:

* Customer testimonials
* Revenue generated
* Closed client examples
* Case studies

Placeholder until available.

---

## Problem Section

Most local businesses already have websites.

Finding businesses that still need one is difficult.

Manual prospecting takes hours.

NoSiteFinder automates discovery.

---

## How It Works

1. Search an industry and location.
2. Find businesses without websites.
3. Prioritize opportunities.
4. Contact prospects.
5. Close clients.

---

## Feature Section

* Website Detection
* Opportunity Scoring
* Lead Lists
* Notes
* Contact Pipeline
* CSV Export

---

## Results Section

Future:

Highlight real users and wins.

Examples:

Closed $2,500 website project.

Generated 75 qualified prospects.

Booked 4 discovery calls.

---

## Pricing Section

Simple pricing.

No enterprise plans.

No complexity.

---

## Design Principles

* Clean
* Minimal
* Modern SaaS
* Professional

Avoid:

* AI-generated illustrations
* Generic stock photos
* Excessive gradients
* Buzzword-heavy copy

Success Metric:

Visitors immediately understand:

Who it is for.

What problem it solves.

Why it is different.
